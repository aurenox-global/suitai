❓ Frequently Asked Questions

🔒 Does SuitAI collect my data?
No. SuitAI runs completely offline. No data is sent to the cloud, no telemetry, no analytics. Your privacy is absolute.

💰 Is it free? Any subscription?
Yes — completely free. No in-app purchases, no hidden fees, no “pro” version.

📄 How do I process PDFs?
Drag any PDF into the app and ask SuitAI to summarize, extract insights, or answer questions about the document.

⚙️ Do I need to install Ollama separately?
No. SuitAI includes built‑in Ollama integration but works autonomously with its own local models.
